# Candidates & Tournament — Make the AI Judge Laugh (office life)

Competition: `u-rama-melon-make-the-ai-judge-laugh-302d0825` ($2).
Gate: one line, 1–200 characters, a single joke about office life.

Judge brief criteria, in weight order:
1. About office life and lands as a joke.
2. Observation true to the office (meetings, email, bosses, coffee, deadlines, printer).
3. Surprising turn — fresh angle or unexpected last word, not a pun or stock line.
4. Tight — no wasted setup, reads cleanly aloud.
5. Original — not a familiar line with nouns swapped.

## Final: C2 vs C12

- **C2:** "I replied-all to "please stop replying all" and the thread is now 47 emails about a birthday cake."
